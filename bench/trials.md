## 350-tps first-batch PP quest (2026-08-14/15)

Goal: largest ctx with pp1 > 350 tok/s and no meaningful TG loss. Outcome:
structurally unreachable - the ~27 tps PP gap between MTP and no-MTP is the
per-ubatch MTP catch-up decode, a fixed cost independent of ctx size.
draft-ubatch consolidation OOMs (16384: ~7 GB; 2048: 878 MiB on ROCm1);
target ub 896 wedges the fit. ngram drafting never fires on novel prose
(acc 0) so it equals no-spec. Pareto frontier at 155k:
