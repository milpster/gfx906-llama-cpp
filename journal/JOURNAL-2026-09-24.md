## E179 - recurrent M-RoPE warning root cause and offline fix

### Runtime evidence
- `log/llama-server-iq6v-f16-f16.log:1126-1136` shows an exact LCP match followed by `154802 after 154801` for 384 rows, `154802 after 154802` for 66 rows, `154829 after 154802` for three rows, and a 25-row DFlash hole fill.
- The first row boundary is aligned with the restored recurrent frontier, which rules out stale LCP state for this request.
- The 384-row and 66-row groups are M-RoPE image embeddings pinned to one temporal coordinate. They are processed by the recurrent graph; repeated temporal metadata does not mean repeated token processing.
- The later text jump reflects the image position span. DFlash skips the pinned image rows by design and zero-fills the scalar positions its draft cache did not receive.

### Root cause and change
- `llama_memory_recurrent::find_slot` checked `last_pos == cell.pos + n_seq_tokens`, a scalar-position invariant that cannot represent M-RoPE image geometry.
- The warning check is now limited to non-M-RoPE ubatches with `!ubatch.is_pos_2d()`. Recurrent state updates and DFlash behavior are unchanged.
- `BUG-GDN-MTMD.md` was corrected: its earlier double-counted-text and skipped-image-state diagnosis was not supported by the current decode path.
