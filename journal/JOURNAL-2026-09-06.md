## E124 - KLD full stats remeasure (mean/median/p95/p99/max, top-1/top-5, logit error)

Method: fresh 6-chunk dumps of BOTH sides (mainline A, fork C),
tool-run KLD for continuity (reproduces E122 exactly: mean 0.025665,

Dump format (from perplexity.cpp, was initially misread as f16):
per position nv=2*((n_vocab+1)/2)+4 uint16 = [scale f32][min_log_prob
f32][n_vocab u16], log_prob = scale*u + min_log_prob; window clamped
to 16 nats below max logit (u step ~2.44e-4 nats).

Results (12282 positions, base=mainline Q=fork):

| metric | KL(base||Q) | KL(Q||base) |
|---|---|---|
| mean | 0.024764 | 0.024533 |
| median | 1.64e-06 | 1.55e-06 |
| p95 | 0.010027 | 0.010157 |
| p99 | 0.312291 | 0.332746 |
| max | 15.773 | 16.007 |

Tool cross-check: p95/p99/top-1 match to 4-5 decimals. Dump-vs-dump
mean/max run ~3.5%/19% below the tool's live numbers because the Q
side's -16-nat window clamp saturates deep-tail entries (per-entry
|e| caps at 16.414 = window width; live tool max 19.6).

Agreement: top-1 98.6484%, top-5 sets identical 73.473%, base top-1
in Q top-5 99.813%. Top-5 SETS are fragile (ranks 2-5 shuffle on
near-ties); containment is the meaningful metric and it is excellent.

Next-token prob: |dp| median 7.6e-6, mean 0.0042, p99 0.089, max
0.999 (one full flip).

Logit error (shift-normalized = log-prob diff; u16 floor 2.4e-4):
full-vocab RMSE 0.0669 (diluted by 248k mostly-identical tail
entries), union-top-100 RMSE 0.495 (rare large flips dominate the
mean-square), median per-position max|e| 0.420, max|e| >= 16.41
(window-saturated). Interpretation: typical entry differences are at
the quantization floor; error concentrates on near-tie candidates.
