## FATTN fix (post-bisect; user direction: speed over ctx, kernel work welcome; plan revised 2026-08-30 after review)
Proven vs hypothesis:
- PROVEN, end-to-end (c 200k): FATTN-on swaps convert -> native q8_0-V tile: buffer 588.65 -> 238.15 (whole compaction, the 250k-ctx feature), TG 11.9 -> 9.3, fill 227.4 -> 202.0, acc .658 -> .646. Path ordering E2E: convert > tile >>> VEC.
- UNPROVEN: raw kernel ordering (acc drifts per path, so e2e deltas mix kernel time with acceptance) and the mechanism (repeated dequant ALU/LSU vs VGPR/occupancy vs LDS vs VMEM vs HBM traffic - the PMU must split these).
- Code-verified mechanism (fattn-tile.cuh / fattn-common.cuh, 2026-08-30 read): gqa 6 dispatches `ncols2 = 2` (fattn-tile.cuh:1540) = 3 head-group blocks per KV head (:1018 block->KV head = head0/gqa_ratio); the q8_0 V dequant runs once per block per KV chunk (:858) inside the KV-chunk loop (:1151) -> each q8 V element is dequantized `ntiles_z_gqa x ceil(Q/32)` = 3x per verify op (Q<=32 = one Q tile of 32 rows: NO per-Q-row scaling) and 36x at a 384-row ubatch. K is f16 in the lane KV config: V-only dequant. Loader = 16x 2B-aligned ushort loads + ~7 ALU/CVT per ushort (:485). Bytes: native ~3.75B/elem total (3 group blocks overlap in L2) vs convert ~9.75B/elem (q8 read + f16 write + 3x f16 reads) - the convert TG win is plausibly ALU/LSU (2/3 of dequant removed), NOT bandwidth: PMU must confirm which. HW: Radeon VII = Vega 20, 60 CU, 16GB HBM2, 1024 GB/s.
